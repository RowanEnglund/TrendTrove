# Dropshipping and Order Fulfillment Specification

## 1. Dropshipping Model

-   **Order Processing**:
    -   When a customer places an order, TrendTrove will automatically process the payment.
    -   The order is then forwarded to the respective supplier.
-   **Supplier Management**:
    -   TrendTrove will maintain a curated list of reliable suppliers.
    -   A system for tracking supplier performance and inventory levels will be in place.
-   **Shipping and Delivery**:
    -   The supplier ships the product directly to the customer.
    -   TrendTrove will provide customers with tracking information to monitor their delivery.

## 2. Customer Support

-   **Order Tracking**: Users can track the status of their orders in real-time within the app and on the website.
-   **Support Tickets**: A support ticket system for handling customer inquiries, returns, and refunds.
-   **Contact Information**: Clear and accessible contact information for customer support.
