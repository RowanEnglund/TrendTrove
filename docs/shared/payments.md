# Payments Specification

## 1. Payment Gateway Integration

- Integrate with a secure and reliable payment gateway (e.g., Stripe, PayPal).
- Support for major credit and debit cards.
- Option to save payment methods for future purchases.

## 2. Pricing and Costs

- Display the total price of the product, including the base price, shipping fees, taxes, and any other applicable charges.
- Transparent breakdown of the total cost at checkout.
- Handle currency conversion for international products and customers.

## 3. Order Placement

- Users can add products to their cart and proceed to checkout.
- Review the order details, including shipping information and payment method, before confirming the purchase.
- Receive an order confirmation email upon successful payment.
