# Products Specification

## 1. Product Discovery

-   **Homepage Feed**: Display a dynamic feed of the latest trending products.
-   **Categorization**: Products will be organized into a wide variety of categories (e.g., electronics, fashion, home goods).
-   **Search and Filter**: Users can search for products and filter them based on category, price range, and other attributes.
-   **Local Sourcing Focus**: The app will prioritize showing products that can be sourced locally to the user.

## 2. Product Details

-   **Product Page**: Each product will have a dedicated page with detailed information, including:
    -   High-quality images and videos.
    -   A comprehensive description.
    -   Specifications and features.
    -   Customer reviews and ratings.
    -   Supplier information.
-   **Pricing**: Display the full price, including shipping, taxes, and any other fees.

## 3. User Preferences

-   **Category Selection**: Users can select their preferred product categories to personalize their feed.
-   **Wishlist**: Users can save products to a wishlist for later viewing.
